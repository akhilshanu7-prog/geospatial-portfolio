01 — ABOUT

Mapping places, understanding processes.

Add profile.jpg

I am a postgraduate in Geoinformatics with an academic background in Geography and a strong interest in GIS, Remote Sensing and spatial data science.

My work focuses on using geospatial technologies to understand environmental processes, natural hazards, urban landscapes and land-resource dynamics.

I am particularly interested in combining satellite remote sensing, GIS, Google Earth Engine, machine learning and spatial modelling to develop practical solutions for geographical problems.
